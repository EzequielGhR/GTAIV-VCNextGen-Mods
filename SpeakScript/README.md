# What it does

Tommy can talk! I mean, that's not new, he's not Claude. What I mean is you can make Tommy talk at will when unarmed and targeting someone.
- Target Someone
- Press NavUP (Up arrow for controllers) to say something positive, usually just a variation of "Thanks" for males, Tommy does not have a lot of positive lines.
- For girls Tommy has more variations but yeah nothing out of the ordinary.
- Press NavDown (Down arrow) to say something negative. Sometimes this will piss off peapole.
- Try doing this while in a fist fight, or maybe try insulting a cop, see what happens.

# How To

Just copy `SpeakScript.cs` into your games folder/scripts
