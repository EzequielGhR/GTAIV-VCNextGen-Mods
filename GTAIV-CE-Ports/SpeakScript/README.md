# What it does

Niko can talk! I mean, that's not new, he's not Claude. What I mean is you can make Niko talk at will when unarmed and targeting someone.
- Target Someone
- While targetting Press LookBack (RS for controllers) to say something positive, usually just a variation of "Thanks" for males, Niko does not have a lot of positive lines. (In GTA4 NavUp opens the phone in contrast with VCNG, so I improvised)
- For girls Niko has more variations but yeah nothing out of the ordinary.
- Press NavDown (Down arrow) to say something negative. Sometimes this will piss off people.
- Try doing this while in a fist fight, or maybe try insulting a cop, see what happens.

# How To

Just copy `SpeakScript.cs` into your games folder/scripts
