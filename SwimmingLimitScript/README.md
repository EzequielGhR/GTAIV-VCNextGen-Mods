# What it does

Well since VC next gen is technically GTA4, that means now Tommy swims. I wanted to keep it kind of faithful to og VC but at the same time who does not want to swim in VC?
- Tommy can still swim, but I decided for an intermediate approach, something like GTA VCS, you have stamina so you cannot just swim around the islands.
- Heavier movements drain more stamina, the order is obvious sprint swimming >> fast swimming >> slow swimming >> floating.
- The stamina appears as a bar on the top left when on water, if it drains, Tommy's life will start to drain too, until he drowns.

# How to

Just copy and paste `SwimmingLimitScript.cs` to your game's folder/scripts.
